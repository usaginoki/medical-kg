# Indonesia

> [!summary] Data available
> Indonesia is the best-covered country in Southeast Asia: two BLEnD cultures, the third-largest dish inventories among priority countries, and Jamu formulae in KNApSAcK, but no recipe corpus with amounts and no processed FCT. Start with [[WorldCuisines]], [[FmLAMA]] and [[BLEnD]] (Q1), global composition sources via [[Global]] until TKPI is processed (Q2), and [[KNApSAcK Family]] (Jamu) + [[CMAUP]] (Q3).

## Foods & dishes
- Inventories: [[WorldCuisines]] 143 dishes (83 Indonesia-only); [[FmLAMA]] 113 (rank 8 of 122); [[World Wide Dishes]] 19 (12 Indonesia-only). Ingredients, no amounts.
- [[BLEnD]]: two files, Indonesia (528 answer clusters) and West Java (414), 105 food questions each.
- Recipes: [[RecipeDB2]] ≈360 (v1, Southeast Asian); [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] 218; [[IndicRecipeNutri]] 46 (Indian sites).

## Ingredients & food composition
No FCT processed. [[Indonesian Food Composition Data (TKPI)]] (official, with local dishes; candidate, priority 2); [[ASEAN Food Composition Database]] (regional 2014; priority 2). Backlog recipes: [[Indonesian Food Recipes]] (14k; priority 3).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **Jamu**: [[KNApSAcK Family]] JAMU holds 5,310 formulae (4,723 unique names) with efficacy text and effect groups from Indonesian registrations; we scraped the name list, 126 formula–herb rows and herb-effect examples.
- [[KNApSAcK Family]] World: 2,127 records (786 edible / 1,341 medicinal).
- [[CMAUP]] 379 plants; [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] 2,211 records, 847 taxa (Java 1,795, Sumatra 167, Moluccas 63); [[NPASS]] 389 pairs; [[HERB]] 95 meta-analyses from Indonesia.
- Global resources without country labels (FooDB, SpiceRx, FlavorDB2, Phenol-Explorer, CTD, HMDB, FoodAtlas, FooDrugs) apply via [[Global]].
- Backlog: [[IJAH Analytics]] (Jamu herb → compound → target → disease; priority 2).

## Gaps
No Indonesian-language recipe corpus processed, no FCT, and Jamu is scraped only as samples; no dataset beyond BLEnD's West Java separates regional cuisines (Padang, Javanese, Balinese, Manadonese).
