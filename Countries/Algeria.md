## Foods & dishes
- [[World Wide Dishes]]: **84 dishes, 75 Algeria-only** (e.g. *tlitli* with chicken, *chakhchoukhet dhfer*, *tajin jelbana*, *shtitha batata*, *shorba beyda*, *hmis* pepper salad, merguez). Every dish has an ingredient list (no amounts) and 83 have something in the `recipe` field (often a video link, e.g. YouTube). Algeria is also one of the six countries in its image-generation test suites.
- [[BLEnD]] (`Algeria`, BLEnD-2024): 105 food questions, 331 food answer clusters. Everyday knowledge in Algerian Arabic, no ingredients.
- [[ArabCulture]]: 271 items, **56 in the Food topic (28 Algeria-specific)** + 7 food-adjacent. Sentence-completion MCQs in Arabic, e.g. msemen with honey and *kesra* with coffee for breakfast, *hmiss* with kesra, couscous with red *marqa* or *leben*, bourek with chorba, *qalb el louz* with tea at iftar.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 28 dishes, only 1 Algeria-only (*Chakhchoukha*); the rest are shared Maghreb/Arab dishes (couscous, sfenj, pastilla, gazelle ankles, dolma, ma'amoul). Ingredients from the text description only.
- [[FmLAMA]]: 10 dishes (200 dish × language rows), e.g. *seffa*, *dobara*, *tamina*, *mtewem*, *harira*; Wikidata ingredient lists, no amounts.
- [[ArSyra Food and Culture]]: Algeria claimed for the commercial full product; counts unknown.
- No Algerian label in [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] or [[RecipeDB2]] (its "Northern Africa" region, 1,611 recipes, is mostly Moroccan).

## Ingredients & food composition
- No Algerian FCT in the vault or `Backlog/`; use [[USDA FoodData Central]] as the fallback, or the (unprocessed) [[FAO INFOODS global databases]].
- [[CMAUP]]: 192 plants on the map (185 scraped), the most of any North African country; this is where plants grow, not use.
- [[KNApSAcK Family]]: Algeria has a World flag page (edible / medicinal species); counts were not scraped.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: only 10 ethnobotany records (10 taxa), mostly tumour/cancer uses from old compilations; no Algerian folk-medicine source.
- [[NPASS]]: 135 species–compound pairs with an Algerian collection site (2nd in North Africa after Morocco).
- Compound → effect chains via the [[Global]] backbones ([[FooDB]] has couscous, dates, fenugreek, okra; no nigella or smen).

## Gaps
- No composition table and no recipe corpus with amounts; the World Wide Dishes ingredient lists are the richest Algerian source.
- Algerian herbal medicine (widely documented in the literature) is almost absent: 10 Duke records.
- Regional and Amazigh (Kabyle, Chaoui, Mozabite) cuisines are not labelled anywhere.
