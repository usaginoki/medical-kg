# Mexico

> [!summary] Data available
> The best-covered country in Latin America (13 datasets): large recipe sets in [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] (6,694), [[RecipeDB2]] (≈14,460, not released) and [[CulinaryDB]] (3,138), 131 [[WorldCuisines]] dishes, a [[BLEnD]] culture, and the third-largest ethnobotany record set in [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] (5,384). There is no Mexican FCT in the vault, so composition falls back to [[USDA FoodData Central]].

## Foods & dishes
- [[RecipeDB2]]: v1 `Mexican` region 14,463 recipes (second after Italian); amounts, steps and nutrition, but the data is not released (we hold samples).
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 6,694 recipes tagged `mexican` (sub-tags `baja` 160, `oaxacan` 72); a further 1,476 `tex-mex` recipes are counted under the US. Ingredients without amounts, steps, nutrition; US uploaders, so many are Mexican-American.
- [[CulinaryDB]]: 3,138 recipes labelled `Mexico` (ingredients, partial amounts, no method).
- [[IndicRecipeNutri]]: 467 recipes labelled `Mexican` (gram weights, nutrition; from Indian sites).
- Dish inventories: [[WorldCuisines]] 131 dishes, 83 Mexico-only (rank 7; `Mexican` cuisine label 113, `Mesoamerican` 2); [[FmLAMA]] 61 dishes (e.g. tostada, pico de gallo, chimichanga); [[World Wide Dishes]] 8 dishes (7 Mexico-only, e.g. tlayuda, sope). Ingredient lists, no amounts.
- [[BLEnD]] `Mexico`: 105 food questions, 563 food answer clusters (among the richest BLEnD food sets).
- Backlog: [[RecetasDeLaAbuela]] (priority 3, open-download): Spanish-language recipes labelled by Spanish-speaking country; per-country counts not known yet.

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national food composition table in the vault or `Backlog/`; use [[USDA FoodData Central]] as the fallback (its SR Legacy has 15 `Restaurant, Latino` and 6 `Restaurant, Mexican` items). [[FooDB]] has a few Tex-Mex / Mexican dish entries (taco, burrito, nachos).
- Plants: [[CMAUP]] 276 plants on the map; [[NPASS]] 126 specimen pairs collected in Mexico.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 5,384 ethnobotany records, 1,480 taxa, the third-largest country set after China and India; this is the main source for Mexican herbal medicine (no dedicated Mexican traditional-medicine database in the vault). [[KNApSAcK Family]] has a World-map page for it (edible/medicinal species records), not scraped, so no count in the vault.
- [[Exposome-Explorer]]: 217 rows (148 concentrations, 52 correlations, 17 reproducibility).
- Compound → effect chains via the [[Global]] backbones ([[FooDB]], [[CTD]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[SpiceRx]]).

## Gaps
- No Mexican FCT and no Spanish-language Mexican recipe corpus with amounts that is released.
- Regional cuisines (Oaxacan, Yucatecan, Poblano, Norteño) are barely labelled (Food.com `oaxacan` 72, `baja` 160).
- Mexican traditional medicine (herbolaria) exists only as Duke records, without a curated plant → indication → dose source.
