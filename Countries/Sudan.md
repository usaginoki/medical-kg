## Foods & dishes
- [[ArabCulture]] (Nile Valley group): 256 items, **57 in the Food topic, 50 of them Sudan-specific** + 4 food-adjacent; e.g. *asida* with *mullah* stew, lentils with bread, *ta'amiya* made from chickpeas (*kabkabeek*), *sha'iriya* (vermicelli) with sugar, dishes cooked on the *saj* or charcoal *manqad*.
- [[World Wide Dishes]]: **28 dishes, 18 Sudan-only** (rank 10 of 99), all with ingredient lists and 22 with a recipe field; many are shared Arab dishes (shawarma, kunafa, *kahk*, *mahshi*, basbousa, *luqeimat*, rice with *mulukhiyah*).
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 10 dishes, none Sudan-only (*shahan ful* and *kisra* shared with South Sudan, *kamounia*, hummus, freekeh).
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 15 recipes tagged `sudanese`. [[RecipeDB2]]: 19 recipes (exact; samples only held).
- [[ArSyra Food and Culture]]: Sudan claimed. No BLEnD file and no FmLAMA dishes.

## Ingredients & food composition
- No Sudanese FCT in the vault or `Backlog/`; [[USDA FoodData Central]] is the fallback ([[FooDB]] has sorghum, millet, okra, hibiscus tea and dates, but no baobab or gum arabic).
- [[CMAUP]]: 128 plants (122 scraped). [[KNApSAcK Family]]: flag page, counts not scraped.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: **351 records (184 taxa)**, by far the most in North Africa; top uses poison, purgative, diuretic, tonic, fever, emetic, laxative; frequent taxa *Aristolochia bracteata*, *Balanites aegyptiaca*, *Boerhaavia diffusa*.
- Compound → effect chains via [[Global]].

## Gaps
- No recipes with amounts, no FCT and no BLEnD file.
- Staples such as *kisra*/*asida* (sorghum), baobab and gum arabic have no composition entry in the vault.
