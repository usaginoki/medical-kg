# Morocco

> [!summary] Data available
> The best-covered Maghreb country and the only one with recipe corpora: [[RecipeDB2]] (≈1,560 recipes with amounts, but only samples held) and [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] (897 user-tagged recipes), plus a [[BLEnD]] file and [[ArabCulture]] (33 Morocco-specific food items). No Moroccan FCT; plant data is comparatively good ([[KNApSAcK Family]] 263 records, [[NPASS]] 248 pairs, [[CMAUP]] 184 plants) and compound effects come from [[Global]].

## Foods & dishes
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈1,560 recipes labelled Morocco (v1, region "Northern Africa" 1,611), with ingredient amounts, steps and nutrition; the data is not released and we hold only sample recipes.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: **897 recipes tagged `moroccan`** (14th of 69 countries), with ingredients (no amounts), steps and %DV nutrition. User tags on an American site, so many tagines and couscous dishes are adaptations.
- [[BLEnD]] (`Arabic_Morocco`, SemEval-2026): 105 food questions but only **199 food answer clusters**, the fewest of all 33 culture files.
- [[ArabCulture]]: 276 items, **54 in the Food topic (33 Morocco-specific)** + 8 food-adjacent; e.g. mint tea with *melwi* or *baghrir*, sfenj with sugar, msemen, *bissara* with olive oil and cumin, chickpeas on couscous.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 25 dishes, 5 Morocco-only (*briouat*, *ahriche*, *mrouzia*, *matbucha*, *qrashel*); the rest shared (couscous, pastilla, baghrir, gazelle ankles, sfenj).
- [[FmLAMA]]: 8 dishes (139 rows), e.g. *chebakia*, *seffa*, *feqqas*, *baghrir*, *loubia*; ingredients, no amounts.
- [[World Wide Dishes]]: 6 dishes, none Morocco-only (gazelle cornets, *griwech*, baghrir, *zelabya*, *m'hancha*); ingredients listed.
- [[ArSyra Food and Culture]]: Morocco claimed for the full product; counts unknown.
- Backlog: [[Kaggle What's Cooking (Yummly)]] (candidate, priority 2) includes a Moroccan cuisine label; [[MENA-150]] (candidate, dish images).

## Ingredients & food composition
- No Moroccan FCT in the vault or `Backlog/`. Use [[USDA FoodData Central]] (or [[FooDB]]) by name matching; the unprocessed [[FAO INFOODS global databases]] may list a national table.
- [[KNApSAcK Family]]: **263 records (154 edible / 109 medicinal)**, the only North African country we scraped. Edible species include *Allium roseum*, *Arisarum vulgare* and argan (*Argania*); medicinal include *Ajuga iva*, *Ammi visnaga*, *Aristolochia longa*, *Artemisia absinthium* and *Argania spinosa*.
- [[CMAUP]]: 184 plants on the map (178 scraped).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[NPASS]]: **248 species–compound pairs** collected in Morocco, the most of any African country.
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 20 records (16 taxa), e.g. warts, aphrodisiac, tumour (*Ruta chalepensis*, *Paronychia argentea*).
- The KNApSAcK medicinal list is the closest thing to a Moroccan herbal pharmacopoeia in the vault; compound → effect chains via [[Global]] ([[FooDB]] has no argan oil, harissa, smen or nigella).

## Gaps
- No Moroccan FCT; recipes with amounts exist only as RecipeDB2 samples.
- No structured Moroccan traditional-medicine (herbalist, *attar*) dataset beyond KNApSAcK's species list.
- Regional cuisines (Fassi, Amazigh, Saharan) are unlabelled; BLEnD's Moroccan file is thin (199 clusters).
