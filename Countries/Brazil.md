## Foods & dishes
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 247 recipes tagged `brazilian` (ingredients without amounts, steps, nutrition).
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈440 recipes labelled Brazil in v1 (South American region 7,176); not released.
- Dish inventories: [[WorldCuisines]] 54 dishes, 28 Brazil-only (`Brazilian` cuisine label 35); [[FmLAMA]] 21 dishes (e.g. coxinha, brigadeiro, quindim); [[World Wide Dishes]] 10 dishes (8 Brazil-only, e.g. vinagrete, pudim). Ingredient lists, no amounts.
- No BLEnD culture; no Portuguese-language recipe corpus.

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national food composition table in the vault or `Backlog/`; [[USDA FoodData Central]] is the fallback; the Brazilian table (TACO) is not yet a candidate.
- Plants: [[CMAUP]] 267 plants on the map; [[NPASS]] 334 specimen pairs collected in Brazil (the largest in the Americas).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 761 ethnobotany records, 457 taxa. [[KNApSAcK Family]] has a World-map page for it (edible/medicinal species records), not scraped, so no count in the vault.
- [[Exposome-Explorer]]: 8 correlation rows only.
- Compound → effect chains via the [[Global]] backbones ([[FooDB]], [[CTD]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[SpiceRx]]).

## Gaps
- No Brazilian FCT (TACO) and no Portuguese-language recipe corpus.
- Regional cuisines (Bahian, Mineiro, Amazonian) are unlabelled.
- Amazonian foods (açaí, cupuaçu, tucupi) need a compound source beyond NPASS specimen records.
