# India

> [!summary] Data available
> India is the best-covered country in South Asia and one of the best in the vault: a 219k-recipe corpus with state labels, amounts and nutrition, a recipe nutrient databank, and three Indian traditional-medicine knowledge bases. Start with [[IndicRecipeNutri]] (Q1), [[Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)]] (Q2) and [[IMPPAT]] + [[GRAYU]] (Q3); the national FCT itself (IFCT 2017) is still unprocessed.

## Foods & dishes
- [[IndicRecipeNutri]]: 171,404 recipes scoped `indian`, 23,769 `indian-adapted` and 868 `indo-fusion`; ingredients with gram weights, cooking-method tags, nutrition and nutrition-derived health tags. Partly accessed (recipe prose and the nutrition enrichment table not taken).
  - **Per state** (`Region`): Kerala 11,527 · Tamil Nadu 11,375 · Punjab 10,502 · West Bengal 6,431 · Maharashtra 5,016 · Karnataka 4,788 · Gujarat 3,677 · Andhra Pradesh 1,862 · Goa 1,545 · Rajasthan 871 · Bihar 771 · Telangana 743 · Jammu & Kashmir 457 · Uttar Pradesh 393 · Odisha 80 · Assam 65 · Himachal Pradesh 52 · Uttarakhand 8 · Nagaland 8 · Manipur 1; macro-regions North India 12,112 and South India 11,579; communities Parsi 149 and Sindhi 306. 135,018 recipes are Pan-Indian (unlabelled).
  - Sub-regional cuisines: Mughlai 403, Mangalorean 378, Hyderabadi 368, Kashmiri 247, Chettinad 237, Awadhi 145, Konkani 98, Indo-Chinese 868.
- [[Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)]]: 1,014 recipes with ingredient amounts and full nutrients, no cooking steps and no state column (Delhi home-science cookbooks, so North/pan-Indian leaning).
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈5,980 India recipes in v1 (region "Indian Subcontinent" 6,464); amounts, steps and nutrition, but the data is not released (we hold 43 API + 375 web records). v2 adds ~10.9k Indian recipes.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 2,708 recipes tagged Indian (steps, nutrition, no amounts; user tags on an American site, many adaptations).
- [[CulinaryDB]] "Indian Subcontinent" 4,058 recipes (mostly TarlaDalal, so largely Indian; ingredients, partial amounts, no method).
- Dish inventories: [[FmLAMA]] 149 dishes; [[WorldCuisines]] 127 dishes (57 India-only); [[World Wide Dishes]] 62 (55 India-only). Ingredients only, no amounts.
- [[BLEnD]]: 105 food questions for **Assam only** (546 answer clusters).

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national FCT processed. [[Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017)]] is a candidate (priority 1; site down). [[Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)]] computes its recipe nutrients from IFCT 2017/2004 values, so it is the usable proxy for now; [[IndicRecipeNutri]] carries ingredient weights and recipe-level nutrition.
- Backlog recipe corpora: [[Indian Food 101]] (255 dishes with state/region; candidate, priority 2), [[6000+ Indian Food Recipes]] (Archana's Kitchen with cuisine, amounts, steps; priority 2), [[INDoRI]] (18 regions, no link; priority 3), [[Khana]] (dish taxonomy, images; priority 3), [[South Asian Recipes with Nutrition]] (possibly synthetic; priority 3).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, Sowa Rigpa, Homeopathy**
  - [[IMPPAT]]: 4,154 Indian medicinal plants, 18,314 phytochemicals, 94,261 plant-part → therapeutic-use links, 1,576 Ayurvedic formulations. Plants per system: Ayurveda 1,328 · Siddha 1,151 · Unani 813 · Sowa Rigpa 325 · Homeopathy 289. No state labels.
  - [[GRAYU]]: Ayurveda-centred graph (full DB 12,743 plants, 1,039 formulations, 13,480 diseases with evidence-typed edges). We hold only an excerpt (terms forbid bulk scraping). Plants per state in the excerpt (query-biased, where plants grow): West Bengal 536 · Maharashtra 468 · Tamil Nadu 432 · Kerala 422 · Chhattisgarh 378 · Andhra Pradesh 362 · Karnataka 355 · Odisha 347 … Lakshadweep 10. Vernacular names (Hindi *Haldi*, Telugu *Pasupu*, Bengali *Halud*) map regional ingredient names.
  - [[CMAUP]] system labels: Indian Folk 681 · Ayurveda 397 · Siddha 367 · Unani 274 · Sowa-Rigpa 153 plants; 1,096 plants on India's map.
  - [[UNaProd]] (Persian medicine, labelled South Asia): the Greco-Arabic humoral tradition behind Unani; many synonyms are tagged Hindi.
- Per-country counts: [[KNApSAcK Family]] 3,685 records (742 edible / 2,943 medicinal); [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] 6,837 records, 1,545 taxa (incl. - Global resources without country labels (FooDB, SpiceRx, FlavorDB2, Phenol-Explorer, CTD, HMDB, FoodAtlas, FooDrugs) apply via [[Global]].
- Backlog: [[FRLHT ENVIS Medicinal Plants Database]] (by state, GRAYU's source; priority 2), [[OSADHI]] (herbs with state and vernacular names; priority 2), [[AyuCaRe]] (Ayurveda case reports; priority 3).

## Gaps
- No processed national FCT (IFCT 2017 site down) and no dietary-intake survey linked to health outcomes.
- 135,018 of 219,384 IndicRecipeNutri recipes have no state; the North-East (Assam 65, Nagaland 8, Manipur 1), Odisha (80) and the populous Hindi belt (Uttar Pradesh 393, Bihar 771) are thin next to Kerala or Tamil Nadu.
- BLEnD covers Assam only; IMPPAT has no state labels; GRAYU is an excerpt pending a bulk-access request.
- Traditional-medicine links are plant → use; there is no food-level Ayurvedic flag (dosha/diet rules per dish).
