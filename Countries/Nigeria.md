# Nigeria

> [!summary] Data available
> The best-covered country in Sub-Saharan Africa for dishes: [[World Wide Dishes]] has 88 community-written dishes (72 Nigeria-only) in Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa, [[FmLAMA]] 43 and [[WorldCuisines]] 32, and [[BLEnD]] has a Northern Nigeria file. There is no recipe corpus with amounts we can use (RecipeDB2 is sample-only) and no national FCT in the vault (the West African FCT is inside the FAO/INFOODS candidate); compounds come from [[CMAUP]] plant occurrence, [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] ethnobotany and the [[Global]] backbone.

## Foods & dishes
- [[World Wide Dishes]]: **88 dishes (72 Nigeria-only)**, the most of any country in the dataset; free-text ingredients, no amounts or method, plus eating context (time of day, occasion, utensils, local-language name). Languages: Yoruba 34, Igbo 13, Hausa 10, English 10; cultures Yoruba 18, Igbo 11. Examples: Edikang Ikong, Abacha, Adalu, suya-style skewered meat, Hausa rice pancake, millet dough balls. Nigeria is one of six countries with a 30-dish image test set and **1,212 community image-review rows** (familiarity, match, offensiveness ratings).
- [[FmLAMA]]: 43 dishes (186 dish×language rows), e.g. Abacha Ugba, Afang, Abak soup, Amala; Wikidata ingredient lists, no amounts.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 32 dishes (18 Nigeria-only), e.g. Ofada rice, Moin moin, Amala, Suya, Draw soup; Wikipedia-style descriptions naming ingredients, no amounts.
- [[BLEnD]] (`Northern_Nigeria`): 105 food questions, 339 food answer clusters. Everyday food knowledge of the Hausa-speaking north, no ingredients; not a national profile.
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈2,520 recipes labelled Nigerian in v1, with amounts, steps and nutrition, but the label looks like a default for most of "Rest Africa" (2,740) and the data is not released (we hold samples only).
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 38 user-tagged recipes (ingredients without amounts, steps, nutrition; Americanised).

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national FCT is in the vault. The [[FAO INFOODS global databases]] candidate (priority 2, directory of 308 tables) includes the FAO/INFOODS **West African Food Composition Table (WAFCT)**, a regional table for the ECOWAS countries including Nigeria; processing it is the most direct way to get local foods (fonio, cowpea, shea, baobab, leafy sauces). Until then, use [[USDA FoodData Central]] by name matching.
- Plant occurrence: [[CMAUP]] 177 plants on the map; [[NPASS]] 24 species–compound pairs from Nigerian collection sites.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 188 ethnobotany records (113 taxa), mostly from the Ayensu reference; uses such as tumour, fever, laxative, vermifuge.
- [[CMAUP]] gives plant → target → disease links for the 177 plants it places in Nigeria; [[KNApSAcK Family]] lists Nigeria on its World map, but African flag pages were not scraped (counts unknown).
- Compound → effect chains come from the global backbone via [[Global]] ([[FooDB]], [[CTD]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[SpiceRx]], [[DDID]] for food–drug interactions).

## Gaps
- No Nigerian recipe corpus with amounts (RecipeDB2 unreleased, label unreliable) and no processed FCT (WAFCT sits inside the FAO/INFOODS candidate).
- No structured Yoruba/Igbo/Hausa herbal-medicine dataset; Duke's 188 records are thin for a population this size.
- Staples such as egusi, ogbono, uziza, iru (locust bean) and palm oil need compound data from literature.
