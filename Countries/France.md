# France

> [!summary] Data available
> One of the best-covered cuisines in the vault: large dish inventories ([[FmLAMA]] 199 dishes, [[WorldCuisines]] 117) and recipe corpora ([[RecipeDB2]] ≈6,380, [[CulinaryDB]] 2,703, [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] 2,268) plus a [[BLEnD]] file, but no French food composition table (CIQUAL is not in the vault). France is also the strongest European country in [[Exposome-Explorer]] (1,273 rows; EPIC, E3N, SU.VI.MAX, NutriNet-Santé), giving real human-cohort evidence for Q3.

## Foods & dishes
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈6,380 recipes labelled French in v1 (region "French" 6,381); amounts, steps and nutrition, but the data is not released (we hold only sample records).
- [[CulinaryDB]]: 2,703 recipes labelled France (ingredients, partial amounts, no method).
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 2,268 recipes tagged `french` (ingredients without amounts, steps, nutrition; user tags on an American site, often adapted).
- [[IndicRecipeNutri]]: 650 recipes with cuisine "French" from Indian recipe sites (gram weights, method tags, nutrition), i.e. French dishes as cooked for an Indian audience.
- Dish inventories: [[FmLAMA]] 199 dishes (2,857 dish × language rows; e.g. aligot, axoa, baeckeoffe); [[WorldCuisines]] 117 dishes (75 France-only; e.g. teurgoule, crêpe); [[World Wide Dishes]] 5 (2 France-only; tarte flambée, bœuf bourguignon). Ingredient lists only, no amounts.
- [[BLEnD]] `French_France` (SemEval-2026): 105 food questions, 482 answer clusters of everyday food knowledge, no ingredients.

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national food composition table for this country is in the vault or `Backlog/`; use [[USDA FoodData Central]] (and [[FooDB]] / [[Phenol-Explorer]] for compounds) by name matching. The French national table (ANSES CIQUAL) is not yet a candidate.
- [[Phenol-Explorer]] (built by INRA with IARC) is French in origin but has no country labels: its 458 foods are generic commodities.
- Plants: [[CMAUP]] 258 plants on the world map for France; [[NPASS]] 41 species–compound pairs collected in France (+ New Caledonia 21, French Guiana 8).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **Human-cohort evidence:** [[Exposome-Explorer]] (hosted by IARC in Lyon) has 1,273 French rows: 261 concentrations, 817 intake–biomarker correlations, 126 biomarker–cancer associations and 69 metabolomic associations. Cohorts include SU.VI.MAX (587 correlation rows), EPIC, E3N and NutriNet-Santé; cancer rows are mostly breast cancer (83) and gastric/oesophageal cancer (18).
- Traditional use: [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] ETHNOBOT has 233 French records (174 taxa); [[KNApSAcK Family]] World lists the country (edible/medicinal species records; counts for European countries were not scraped).
- No French herbal / phytotherapy database is in the vault.
- Global backbone via [[Global]]: [[FooDB]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[CTD]], [[SpiceRx]], [[DDID]] for food–drug interactions.

## Gaps
- No French FCT (CIQUAL) and no French-language recipe corpus with amounts that we can download (RecipeDB2 is unreleased).
- Regional cuisines (Alsace, Brittany, Provence, Basque) exist only as dish names in FmLAMA/WorldCuisines, without amounts.
