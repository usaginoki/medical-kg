## Foods & dishes
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈2,140 recipes labelled Spanish (region "Spanish and Portuguese" 2,844); amounts, steps, nutrition, not released.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 1,072 recipes tagged `spanish` (steps, nutrition, no amounts).
- [[CulinaryDB]]: 816 Spain recipes (ingredients, partial amounts). [[IndicRecipeNutri]]: 115 "Spanish" recipes from Indian sites.
- Dish inventories: [[FmLAMA]] 124 dishes (1,159 rows, incl. 42 rows labelled Catalonia); [[WorldCuisines]] 87 dishes (49 Spain-only; paella, arròs negre, fideuà, horchata); [[World Wide Dishes]] 4 (3 Spain-only; churro, fideuà, calçotada). Ingredient lists only.
- [[BLEnD]]: `Spain` (BLEnD-2024; 105 food questions, 503 answer clusters) and `Basque_BasqueCountry` (SemEval-2026; 413 clusters), everyday food knowledge without ingredients.

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national food composition table for this country is in the vault or `Backlog/`; use [[USDA FoodData Central]] (and [[FooDB]] / [[Phenol-Explorer]] for compounds) by name matching. The Spanish table (BEDCA) is not a candidate. [[RecetasDeLaAbuela]] (candidate, open-download) is a recipe set for Spanish-speaking countries.
- Plants: [[CMAUP]] 281 plants on the map; [[NPASS]] 63 species–compound pairs collected in Spain.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **Human-cohort evidence:** [[Exposome-Explorer]] 1,021 Spanish rows (688 concentrations, 231 correlations, 63 reproducibility, 39 metabolomic). Cohorts: EPIC (84 concentration and 101 correlation rows), INMA, ENCAT, EUREYE and **PREDIMED** (33 metabolomic-association rows).
- Traditional use: [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] ETHNOBOT 748 records (642 taxa), the most for any European country; [[KNApSAcK Family]] World lists the country (edible/medicinal species records; counts for European countries were not scraped).
- Global backbone via [[Global]]: [[FooDB]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[CTD]], [[SpiceRx]], [[DDID]] for food–drug interactions.

## Gaps
- No Spanish FCT (BEDCA) and no Spanish-language recipe corpus with amounts in hand.
- Regional cuisines beyond the Basque BLEnD file (Catalan, Galician, Andalusian) only as dish names.
