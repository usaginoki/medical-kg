# Iran

> [!summary] Data available
> Well covered for dishes and uniquely strong for traditional medicine: [[WorldCuisines]] has 65 dishes (32 Iran-only), [[FmLAMA]] 22, [[BLEnD]] a full culture file (105 food questions), and [[UNaProd]] (3,413 Persian materia-medica monographs with Mizaj and actions) is the only structured hot/cold food-temperament source in the vault. The missing piece is composition: the [[Iranian Food Composition Table]] is still a backlog candidate.

## Foods & dishes
- [[WorldCuisines]]: **65 dishes, 32 Iran-specific** (rank 17 of all countries).
- [[BLEnD]] (`Iran`, BLEnD-2024): 105 food questions, 466 food answer clusters. Everyday food knowledge by native annotators.
- [[FmLAMA]]: 22 dishes (322 dish × language rows), ingredient lists without amounts.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 252 recipes tagged `iranian-persian` (ingredients, steps, nutrition; user-tagged, often Americanised).
- [[RecipeDB2]]: Iranian/Persian recipes exist but sit in "Rest Middle Eastern" (≈2,300) without a country label.
- Not in [[ArabCulture]] (Arab countries only).

## Ingredients & food composition
- No Iranian FCT in the vault. Candidate: [[Iranian Food Composition Table]] (candidate, registration; only via EuroFIR FoodEXplorer).
- Until then: [[USDA FoodData Central]] / [[FooDB]] by ingredient name; Food.com gives per-recipe nutrition only for its user recipes.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[UNaProd]]: **3,413 monographs** of Persian traditional medicine (*Makhzan al-Advieh*): 2,516 herbal, 426 animal, 349 mineral, 116 compound. Each has Mizaj (hot/cold × wet/dry, degree), actions, organs, diseases treated, adverse effects, correctives and dose. Many entries are everyday foods (saffron, black seed, fenugreek, pomegranate, dates, walnut). We hold the full index and 38 full monographs; herbal entries link to [[CMAUP]] by scientific name.
- [[CMAUP]]: 140 plants (137 scraped). [[KNApSAcK Family]]: 150 records (60 edible / 90 medicinal). [[NPASS]]: 50 species–compound pairs with Iranian collection sites. [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 100 records / 69 taxa, mostly secondary compilations.
- [[Exposome-Explorer]]: 138 values (14 concentrations, 124 intake–biomarker correlations; Golestan cohort, fruit/vegetable intake ↔ plasma vitamin C, β-carotene, retinol).
- Candidates: [[IrGO]] (candidate, open-download; Persian-medicine ontology of drug actions, used by UNaProd) and [[PHCD (Persian Herbal Constituents)]] (candidate, unavailable, preprint only).
- For compound → effect chains use the [[Global]] backbones ([[FooDB]], [[CTD]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[SpiceRx]], [[DDID]] for food–drug interactions); [[FooDB]] has **no nigella/black seed, sumac, camel milk, za'atar, ghee or labneh**.

## Gaps
- No food composition data for Iranian dishes or ingredients.
- UNaProd is only partly scraped (38 of 3,413 full monographs); Mizaj claims are traditional, not clinical evidence.
- No Iranian recipe corpus with amounts (RecipeDB2 has no Iran label).
