# China

> [!summary] Data available
> China is one of the two best-covered countries in the vault (with Japan): a 1.5M-recipe Chinese-language corpus, dish inventories, and four processed TCM databases with symptoms, diseases, clinical trials and herb–drug interactions. Start with [[XiaChuFang Recipe Corpus]] and [[WorldCuisines]] (Q1) and [[SymMap]], [[HERB]], [[TM-MC]] and [[DDID]] (Q3); the China FCT (Q2) is still only in the Backlog.

## Foods & dishes
- [[XiaChuFang Recipe Corpus]]: 1,520,327 Chinese-language home recipes (64,742 downloaded, 4.3%); ingredients, partial amounts, steps; no province field. Regional tags are rare in our subset: Cantonese 88, Sichuan 75, Hunan 9, Northeastern 9, Xinjiang 5, Yunnan 3. Health tags: weight loss 1,121, infants 511, baby food 108, 养生 38, pregnancy 9, diabetes 2.
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈5,820 recipes "Chinese and Mongolian" (v1); amounts, steps, nutrition, not released.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 2,008 recipes (steps; user tags, many Americanised).
- [[CulinaryDB]]: 941 recipes (ingredients, partial amounts).
- [[IndicRecipeNutri]]: Chinese 692 plus Indo-Chinese 868 (Indian adaptations).
- Inventories: [[WorldCuisines]] 177 dishes (109 China-only); [[FmLAMA]] 103; [[World Wide Dishes]] 8 (6 China-only).
- [[BLEnD]]: 105 food questions, 485 answer clusters.

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national FCT processed: [[China Food Composition Tables]] (print, 6th ed.; candidate, priority 2).
- Backlog: [[China Health and Nutrition Survey (CHNS)]] (diet recalls linked to health outcomes; priority 1), [[FoodieQA]] (14 regional cuisines; priority 2), [[FoodEarth (FoodSky)]] (food + dietetics instructions; priority 2), [[ChineseFoodNet]] (dish images; priority 3).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **TCM** (all processed, all direct or linkable body effects):
  - [[SymMap]]: 698 Chinese Pharmacopoeia herbs → 2,285 TCM symptoms → 1,148 modern-medicine symptoms → 14,086 diseases; 26,035 ingredients.
  - [[HERB]]: 6,892 herbs, 6,743 formulae, 44,595 ingredients, 8,558 clinical trials and 8,032 meta-analyses (2,981 meta-analyses from China). Its trial table mixes foods with materia medica (green tea 67, pomegranate 42, turmeric 32, garlic 26…).
  - [[TM-MC]]: 572 of 649 materials have Chinese names; material → compound (PMID) → protein → disease.
  - [[DDID]]: 1,016 of 1,068 herbs carry Chinese/Pinyin/HERB ids; 23,950 food/herb–drug interactions, 270 foods.
  - [[CMAUP]]: 2,490 TCM plants; 1,726 plants on China's map.
- Per-country counts: [[KNApSAcK Family]] 5,090 records (1,643 edible / 3,447 medicinal); [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] 13,469 records, 1,559 taxa (incl. Hunan 3,720, Hainan, Tibet); [[NPASS]] 4,118 pairs; [[Exposome-Explorer]] 2,000 rows (Shanghai cohorts, 100 cancer rows).
- Global resources without country labels (FooDB, SpiceRx, FlavorDB2, Phenol-Explorer, CTD, HMDB, FoodAtlas, FooDrugs) apply via [[Global]].
- Backlog: [[China Medicine-Food Homology List]] (official 药食同源 list, the missing food flag for TCM herbs; priority 1), [[TCMBank]], [[TCMSP]], [[ETCM]] (priority 2), [[BATMAN-TCM]], [[ChinaTCM-Cuisine]] (dish → TCM efficacy, only 100 items; priority 3).

## Gaps
- No processed FCT and no intake survey (CHNS in the Backlog).
- Sub-national cuisine is almost unlabelled: XiaChuFang has no province field and only ~200 regional tags in our subset; only 4.3% of the corpus is downloaded.
- None of the TCM databases flags which herbs are foods; the medicine-food homology list is needed to join them to recipes.
