## Foods & dishes
- [[Kyrgyzstan Food Composition Table]]: **11 cooked national dishes** with standardized recipes (162 recipe ingredient lines, grams raw and edible) and a short prose method: beshbarmak, dymdama, plov with barberry, 3 manty variants, oromo, lagman, shorpo, mastava, bean soup. Composition is calculated.
- [[Central Asian Digital Visual Food Atlas]]: 115 regional items (95 foods at 3 portion weights in g, 20 beverages in ml), with local names in Kazakh, Uzbek, Kyrgyz, Tajik and Turkmen for 83–85 items; no per-item country, no nutrients.
- [[Central Asian Food Dataset]]: images only, no per-image country. CAFD 42 national dish classes (16,402 images on HF); CAFSD 21,306 meal-scene photos, 239 classes, 69,865 boxes.
- [[FmLAMA]]: 7 dishes (125 rows). [[WorldCuisines]]: 7 dishes, 0 Kyrgyz-only. None in [[World Wide Dishes]].

## Ingredients & food composition
- **Own FCT:** [[Kyrgyzstan Food Composition Table]] (2022, EuroFIR-standard, CC BY 4.0): 41 raw foods, strongly local: 21 fruits (wild apple *Malus sieversii*, sea buckthorn, barberry), 6 milks (koumiss, dried koumiss, mare, cow, yak, hainak), beef/horse/yak fats, chuchuk, bozo, Ozgon rice, honey. Proximates, sugars/fibre, 8 minerals, 7 vitamins; many mineral cells are blank.
