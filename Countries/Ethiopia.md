## Foods & dishes
- [[BLEnD]] (`Ethiopia`): 105 food questions, **317 food answer clusters**: everyday knowledge (what people eat for breakfast, holidays, street food), no ingredients.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 88 recipes tagged Ethiopian (ingredients without amounts, steps, nutrition; often Americanised injera/wat).
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈140 recipes labelled Ethiopian (v1; amounts, steps, nutrition), not released, we hold samples.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 15 dishes (4 Ethiopia-only: gored gored, kitfo, tihlo, tibs); Wikipedia-style descriptions naming ingredients, no amounts.
- [[FmLAMA]]: 4 dishes (31 rows), e.g. wat, quanta firfir, ga'at, tere siga; Wikidata ingredient lists, no amounts.
- No Ethiopian dishes in [[World Wide Dishes]].

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national FCT is in the vault or `Backlog/`. The [[FAO INFOODS global databases]] candidate (priority 2, directory of 308 tables) is where a national or regional table would be found. Fallback: [[USDA FoodData Central]] by name matching.
- Staples such as teff, enset, niger seed and berbere spices will match USDA or [[FooDB]] only partly.
- Plant occurrence: [[CMAUP]] 141 plants; [[NPASS]] 174 pairs (51 compounds, but 1 species).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 82 ethnobotany records (53 taxa), mostly Economic Botany (Eb33) references and Uphof; uses such as tapeworm/taenifuge, stomach ache.
- [[CMAUP]] plant → disease links for 141 plants; [[KNApSAcK Family]] lists Ethiopia on its World map, but African flag pages were not scraped (counts unknown).
- Compound → effect chains come from the global backbone via [[Global]] ([[FooDB]], [[CTD]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[SpiceRx]], [[DDID]] for food–drug interactions).

## Gaps
- No Ethiopian FCT processed and no dish-level dataset beyond ~20 dishes.
- Orthodox fasting diets (a large share of the year without animal products) are not flagged in any dataset.
- Eritrean/Tigrayan and Oromo food are not separated.
