# South Korea

> [!summary] Data available
> South Korea is well covered for composition and traditional medicine (RDA FCT with 3,366 foods, TM-MC built on Korean medicine) but weak on recipes, which come only from English-language corpora. Start with [[Korean Food Composition Table]] (Q2), [[TM-MC]] (Q3), and [[WorldCuisines]] / [[RecipeDB2]] for dishes (Q1); dish-level nutrients (MFDS) and KNHANES are in the Backlog.

## Foods & dishes
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈660 recipes "Korean" (v1; region 668); amounts, steps, nutrition, not released.
- [[CulinaryDB]] 301 recipes; [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] 253; [[IndicRecipeNutri]] 96 (Indian sites).
- Inventories: [[WorldCuisines]] 69 dishes (54 Korea-only; 66 are labelled `Korea`); [[FmLAMA]] 12.
- [[BLEnD]]: 105 food questions (South_Korea), 366 answer clusters.

## Ingredients & food composition
- [[Korean Food Composition Table]] (RDA DB 10.4): 3,366 foods × 132 components, incl. 18 kimchi types, red and white ginseng, jang (doenjang, gochujang, ganjang), regional rice varieties. About 411 foods borrow values (USDA 207, Japan 204).
- Backlog: [[MFDS Food Nutrient DB (Korea)]] (prepared Korean dishes, which the RDA table lacks; priority 2), [[KNHANES]] (dish-level intake + biomarkers; priority 1), [[Korean Traditional Knowledge Portal]] (traditional foods and medicine; priority 2), [[NIFS Fisheries Nutrient DB (Korea)]] (seafood; priority 3), [[AI Hub Korean Food Images]] (foreign access restricted; priority 3).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **Korean medicine**: [[TM-MC]] names all 649 materials in Korean (454 in the Korean pharmacopoeia) and draws its prescriptions from Korean-medicine textbooks and the *Donguibogam*; food pairings such as ginger + jujube appear throughout (ginger in 489 of 2,535 prescriptions).
- Per-country counts: [[KNApSAcK Family]] 442 records (85 edible / 357 medicinal); [[CMAUP]] 192 plants; [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] 12 records; [[NPASS]] 500 pairs; [[Exposome-Explorer]] 749 rows (8 cancer rows); [[HERB]] 90 meta-analyses from Korea.
- Global resources without country labels (FooDB, SpiceRx, FlavorDB2, Phenol-Explorer, CTD, HMDB, FoodAtlas, FooDrugs) apply via [[Global]].

## Gaps
No Korean-language recipe corpus; no processed dish-level composition (MFDS) or intake-plus-biomarker survey (KNHANES); Korean medicine is covered only at material level, without a food-as-medicine flag.
