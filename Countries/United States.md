# United States

> [!summary] Data available
> The best-covered country in the vault by volume, and the default culture of several "global" resources: [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] (31,596 US-tagged recipes), [[CulinaryDB]] (16,118, 35% of the corpus) and [[USDA FoodData Central]] (the only national FCT processed for the Americas) are all American at their core. Start with Food.com (Q1), USDA FDC (Q2) and [[Exposome-Explorer]] (7,553 US study rows, Q3); treat anything labelled "global" in these sources as US-default.

## Foods & dishes
> [!warning] US-default bias
> Three backbone datasets are American first and "world" second. [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] is a US website: 31,596 US-tagged recipes against 7,410 for the next country (Italy), and its "ethnic" tags are often Americanised adaptations. [[CulinaryDB]] labels 16,118 of 45,772 recipes `USA` (35%). [[USDA FoodData Central]] values come from US store and farm samples. When the agent answers for another culture, it should not fall back on these US defaults silently.

- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 31,596 US recipes (ingredients without amounts, steps, %DV nutrition, user reviews). Sub-national tags: `american` 31,179 · `southern-united-states` 7,114 · `tex-mex` 1,476 · `cajun` 1,206 · `creole` 855 · `hawaiian` 444 · `amish-mennonite` 214 · `soul` 210 · `native-american` 164 · `pennsylvania-dutch` 164; region tag `north-american` 48,479 (includes Canada and Mexico).
- [[CulinaryDB]]: 16,118 `USA` recipes (ingredients, partial amounts, no method).
- [[IndicRecipeNutri]]: 12,718 recipes labelled `American` (gram weights, method tags, nutrition), scraped mostly from Indian sites, so often Indian-style takes on American dishes.
- [[RecipeDB2]]: v1 region `US` 5,031 recipes (the country search gives ≈10,900 but also matches e.g. "Russian"); amounts, steps and nutrition, but the data is not released (we hold samples).
- Dish inventories: [[FmLAMA]] 285 dishes (rank 1 in the dataset); [[WorldCuisines]] 216 dishes, 134 US-only (rank 1); [[World Wide Dishes]] 30 dishes (24 US-only), plus a separate 30-dish US test set. Ingredient lists, no amounts.
- [[BLEnD]] `US`: 105 food questions, 518 food answer clusters (everyday food knowledge, no ingredients).

## Ingredients & food composition
- **National FCT:** [[USDA FoodData Central]] (CC0): Foundation 469 foods with 170,469 nutrient values; SR Legacy 7,793 foods with 644,125 values. It includes an `American Indian/Alaska Native Foods` category (165 items, e.g. bearded seal meat) and a few restaurant items by cuisine (Chinese 15, Latino 15, Mexican 6, Italian 5). Branded Foods and FNDDS/Survey foods were not downloaded.
- [[RecipeDB2]] ingredients carry USDA SR Legacy `ndb_id`, and [[FoodKG]] (Backlog) links recipes to USDA, so US composition is the de facto key for most recipe nutrition in the vault.
- Backlog: [[USDA Flavonoid, Isoflavone and Proanthocyanidin Databases]] (priority 2, fills the flavonoid gap in FDC); [[NIH DSLD]] (supplement labels, priority 3).
- Plants: [[CMAUP]] 445 plants on the US map; [[NPASS]] 94 specimen pairs collected in the US (+ Guam 25, Puerto Rico 11, American Samoa 9).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **Human evidence:** [[Exposome-Explorer]] has more US study rows than any other country: 7,553 (3,070 concentrations, 2,932 intake–biomarker correlations, 471 cancer associations, 160 metabolomic associations).
- **Traditional / folk use:** [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] 4,964 ethnobotany records, 1,420 taxa (Hawaii, Puerto Rico and Guam are folded into the US); USDA FDC's Alaska Native foods cover Indigenous diets on the composition side. [[KNApSAcK Family]] has a World-map page for it (edible/medicinal species records), not scraped, so no count in the vault.
- Backlog for supplements and food–drug interactions, a US-regulated area: [[NaPDI Center]] (clinical natural-product–drug interaction studies, priority 2), [[iDISK]] (supplement ingredient–disease–drug KG, priority 2), [[NIH DSLD]].
- Compound → effect chains via the [[Global]] backbones ([[FooDB]], [[CTD]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[SpiceRx]]).

## Gaps
- Regional and ethnic American cuisines (Southern, Cajun, Tex-Mex, Hawaiian, soul food) exist only as user tags in Food.com; no recipe set has amounts plus a region label for them.
- Native American and Alaska Native diets: 164 Food.com recipes and 165 FDC foods; no ethnobotany source specific to US tribes beyond Duke's mixed records.
- The US-default bias above means "global" recipe and composition statistics should be re-weighted before being used for other cultures.
