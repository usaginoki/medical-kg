# Japan

> [!summary] Data available
> Japan is one of the two best-covered countries in the vault: an official FCT, a government dish collection for all 47 prefectures with amounts and steps, a large dish inventory, and strong human-biomarker evidence. Start with [[Our Regional Cuisines (Japan MAFF)]] (Q1), [[Standard Tables of Food Composition in Japan]] (Q2) and [[Exposome-Explorer]] plus [[TM-MC]] / [[CMAUP]] Kampo labels (Q3); the 1.7M-recipe [[NII Cookpad Dataset]] needs an application.

## Foods & dishes
- [[Our Regional Cuisines (Japan MAFF)]]: 1,355 regional dishes (885 in English) with ingredients, amounts and steps, 19–30 per prefecture across all 47 (e.g. Tokyo 30, Kanagawa 19, Okinawa 30 with distinct Ryukyu dishes; Ainu influence in Hokkaido). No nutrition; the English file lacks the prefecture field.
- [[NII Cookpad Dataset]]: ~1,715,000 Japanese recipes, ~36,000 meals, ~10M reviews; ingredients, amounts, steps. On request (signed contract), not accessed; no prefecture field.
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈2,040 recipes (v1); [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] 851; [[CulinaryDB]] 580; [[IndicRecipeNutri]] 118 (Indian sites).
- Inventories: [[FmLAMA]] 189 dishes (rank 4 of 122); [[WorldCuisines]] 182 (155 Japan-only); [[World Wide Dishes]] 23 (all Japan-only).
- [[BLEnD]]: 105 food questions (Japanese_Japan), 446 answer clusters.

## Ingredients & food composition
- [[Standard Tables of Food Composition in Japan]] (MEXT 8th ed. 2023): 2,538 foods × energy, macros, 15 minerals, vitamins, plus amino-acid (1,999), fatty-acid (1,967) and carbohydrate (1,101) tables; 471 fish and shellfish, 58 algae, soy, miso, tsukemono, wagashi. English names joined for 2,184 foods.
- Backlog: [[Rakuten Recipe Dataset]] (~440k recipes; NII application; priority 3), [[Cookpad Image Dataset]] (priority 3), [[KNApSAcK YAKUZEN  DietNavi]] (Japanese medicinal cuisine linked to metabolites; priority 2).

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **Kampo**: [[TM-MC]] 195 materials with Japanese names (192 in the Japanese pharmacopoeia); [[CMAUP]] Kampo 91 plants; [[KNApSAcK Family]] KAMPO (336 formulae, 2012; not scraped). [[KampoDB]] (predicted targets) is a candidate (priority 2).
- Per-country counts: [[KNApSAcK Family]] 3,492 records, mostly edible (2,835 edible / 657 medicinal); [[CMAUP]] 271 plants; [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] 759 records, 288 taxa; [[NPASS]] 376 pairs.
- [[Exposome-Explorer]]: 2,351 rows, second only to the US (JPHC cohort, 87 cancer rows): food intake → biomarker → cancer evidence in a Japanese population.
- Global resources without country labels (FooDB, SpiceRx, FlavorDB2, Phenol-Explorer, CTD, HMDB, FoodAtlas, FooDrugs) apply via [[Global]].

## Gaps
- The regional dishes (MAFF) have no nutrition; they must be joined to the MEXT FCT by ingredient.
- The large recipe corpora (Cookpad, Rakuten) need institutional applications.
- Kampo is thin: no processed Kampo formula → indication database.
