## Foods & dishes
- [[FmLAMA]]: **105 dishes** (1,203 dish × language rows), ingredient lists without amounts.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 69 dishes, 21 Turkey-specific (rank 16).
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈420 recipes labelled Turkish (amounts, steps, nutrition); we hold only samples.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 286 user-tagged recipes (ingredients, steps, nutrition; no amounts).
- [[World Wide Dishes]]: 1 dish.
- Not in [[BLEnD]] or [[ArabCulture]].
- Candidates: [[Turkish Recipe Dataset]] (candidate, 3.3k Turkish recipes) and [[Turkish Cuisine QA]] (candidate, synthetic QA over 2.7k dishes).

## Ingredients & food composition
- No Turkish FCT processed. Candidate: [[TürKomp]] (candidate, open-web national FCDB).
- Until then: [[USDA FoodData Central]] / [[FooDB]] by ingredient name.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 4,815 records / 824 taxa, but 4,664 come from Steinmetz's multilingual codex and may reflect **Turkish-language plant names** rather than documented Turkish use (e.g. *Corekotu* = *Nigella sativa*: carminative, digestive).
- [[KNApSAcK Family]]: **569 records** (201 edible / 368 medicinal), the most in the Middle East. [[CMAUP]]: 288 plants (283 scraped). [[NPASS]]: 315 species–compound pairs.
- [[Exposome-Explorer]]: 12 concentration values.
- For compound → effect chains use the [[Global]] backbones ([[FooDB]], [[CTD]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[SpiceRx]], [[DDID]] for food–drug interactions); [[FooDB]] has **no nigella/black seed, sumac, camel milk, za'atar, ghee or labneh**.

## Gaps
- No composition data until TürKomp is processed.
- No everyday food-knowledge QA (no BLEnD file).
- Turkish ethnobotany in Duke is probably naming, not use; no structured Turkish folk-medicine source.
