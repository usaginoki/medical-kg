## Foods & dishes

Examples: bobotie, malva pudding, smoorsnoek, curried fish, tipsy tart, morogo, umngqusho. ## Gaps
- No South African FCT (the SAFOODS tables are not in the vault or Backlog) and no recipe corpus with amounts.
- Zulu, Xhosa and Sotho food is thin next to Afrikaans and Sepedi in World Wide Dishes.
- No structured dataset of Southern African traditional medicine (muthi) despite high plant coverage.
