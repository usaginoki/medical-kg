# Australia

> [!summary] Data available
> Well covered for Anglo-Australian food: recipes from [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] (2,845), [[RecipeDB2]] (≈4,680, samples only) and [[CulinaryDB]] (494, shared with NZ), a [[BLEnD]] file (504 food answer clusters) and dish lists. Australia is also the only Oceania country with diet–biomarker–cancer evidence in [[Exposome-Explorer]] (471 rows). No Australian FCT is in the vault, and Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander foods and medicine are missing.

## Foods & dishes
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈4,680 recipes labelled Australia (v1 region "Australian" 5,823, which also holds New Zealand), with amounts, steps and nutrition; data not released, samples only held.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: **2,845 recipes tagged `australian`** (5th of 69 countries), ingredients without amounts, steps, %DV nutrition.
- [[CulinaryDB]]: "Australia & NZ" 494 recipes (ingredients, partial amounts, no method), not split by country.
- [[BLEnD]] (`English_Australia`, SemEval-2026): 105 food questions, 504 food answer clusters.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 32 dishes, 7 Australia-only (lamington, neenish tart, Chiko Roll, pie floater, prawn roll, cheesymite scroll, caramel tart); many shared with the UK/NZ (Anzac biscuit, crumpet, rum ball).
- [[FmLAMA]]: 14 dishes (95 rows), e.g. Anzac biscuit, Halal snack pack, frog cake, White Christmas, chocolate crackles, zucchini slice.
- [[World Wide Dishes]]: 2 dishes, both Australia-only (fairy bread, lamingtons).

## Ingredients & food composition
- No Australian FCT (Food Standards Australia New Zealand's AFCD/AUSNUT) in the vault or `Backlog/`; [[USDA FoodData Central]] is the closest analogue for a largely Anglo diet. [[FooDB]] has macadamia nut.
- [[CMAUP]]: 283 plants on the map (the most in Oceania). [[KNApSAcK Family]]: flag page, counts not scraped.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- [[Exposome-Explorer]]: **471 data rows** (226 concentrations, 165 intake–biomarker correlations, 80 biomarker–cancer associations) from Australian cohorts; the strongest human-evidence link in the region.
- [[NPASS]]: 216 species–compound pairs collected in Australia.
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 88 records (77 taxa), mostly poison, cancer, wart and fish-poison uses from old compilations; almost no bush medicine.
- Global backbone via [[Global]].

## Gaps
- No AFCD/AUSNUT food composition table (a clear candidate to add).
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander bush foods and medicine are absent from all datasets.
- Recipe labels are user tags or unreleased (RecipeDB2); multicultural Australian cooking is not separated.
