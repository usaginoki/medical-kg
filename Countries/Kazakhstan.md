## Foods & dishes
- [[Central Asian Food Dataset]]: images only, no per-image country. CAFD 42 national dish classes (16,402 images on HF); CAFSD 21,306 meal-scene photos, 239 classes, 69,865 boxes. Class names are Kazakh transliterations (beshbarmak, kuyrdak, kazy-karta, nauryz-kozhe, shelpek, irimshik, kurt, kymyz-kymyran, taba-nan); in the paper kazy-karta is 6.9% of meat instances, kurt 15.7% and kymyz 15.6% of dairy instances.
- [[Central Asian Digital Visual Food Atlas]]: 115 regional items (95 foods at 3 portion weights in g, 20 beverages in ml), with local names in Kazakh, Uzbek, Kyrgyz, Tajik and Turkmen for 83–85 items; no per-item country, no nutrients.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 10 dishes, 1 Kazakhstan-specific. [[FmLAMA]]: 5 dishes (94 rows). [[World Wide Dishes]]: 2 dishes, none Kazakh-only.
- Not in [[BLEnD]], and no recipe corpus has a Kazakh or Central Asian label.

## Ingredients & food composition
- No Kazakh FCT. The [[Kyrgyzstan Food Composition Table]] (11 national dishes shared across Central Asia: beshbarmak, plov, manty, lagman, shorpo, mastava) can be combined with the atlas portion weights to estimate nutrients per plate.
- Candidates: [[ISSAI LLM for Dietary Recommendation System]] (candidate, open-download; Kazakhstan dietary LLM data), [[ISSAI Food Portion Benchmark]] (candidate, open-download), [[ISSAI Global Gastronomic Culinary Dataset]] (candidate, CAFD/CAFSD extension), [[FooDI-ML]] (candidate, menu-item names incl. Kazakhstan).
