# United Kingdom

## Foods & dishes
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈4,340 recipes (English 2,840 · Scottish 1,120 · Welsh 200 · UK 180); amounts, steps, nutrition, not released.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 2,249 recipes (`english` 1,744, `scottish` 715, `welsh` 203; steps and nutrition, no amounts).
- [[CulinaryDB]]: "British Isles" 1,075 recipes, shared with [[Ireland]] (ingredients, partial amounts).
- [[IndicRecipeNutri]]: 950 "British" recipes from Indian sites (gram weights, method tags, nutrition).
- Dish inventories: [[WorldCuisines]] 160 dishes (107 UK-only; Yorkshire pudding, bannock, figgy pudding; England/Scotland/Wales merged); [[FmLAMA]] 133 dishes (1,759 rows; e.g. Arbroath smokie, Bakewell pudding); [[World Wide Dishes]] 8 (7 UK-only; toad in the hole, Sunday roast, full English). Ingredient lists only.
- [[BLEnD]] `UK` (BLEnD-2024): 105 food questions, 461 answer clusters.

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national food composition table for this country is in the vault or `Backlog/`; use [[USDA FoodData Central]] (and [[FooDB]] / [[Phenol-Explorer]] for compounds) by name matching. McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods (CoFID) is not a candidate yet.
- Plants: [[CMAUP]] 159 plants on the map.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **Human-cohort evidence:** [[Exposome-Explorer]] 795 UK rows (124 concentrations, 458 correlations, 110 cancer associations, 98 metabolomic). EPIC accounts for 108 of the cancer rows (overall mostly prostate 54 and breast 32) and 210 correlations; TwinsUK (76) and MEDE (19) give food → metabolite links; Whitehall and NDNS add intake–biomarker correlations.
- Traditional use: [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] ETHNOBOT 296 records (230 taxa); [[KNApSAcK Family]] World lists the country (edible/medicinal species records; counts for European countries were not scraped).
- Global backbone via [[Global]]: [[FooDB]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[CTD]], [[SpiceRx]], [[DDID]] for food–drug interactions.

## Gaps
- No UK FCT (CoFID) and no downloadable UK recipe corpus with amounts.
- Scottish, Welsh and Northern Irish cuisines are merged into the UK in most datasets (only RecipeDB2 and Food.com keep sub-labels).
