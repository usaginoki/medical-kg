## Foods & dishes
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈2,580 recipes labelled German (region "Deutschland" 4,323, which also includes Austria and Switzerland); amounts, steps, nutrition, not released.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 1,377 recipes tagged `german` (steps, nutrition, no amounts).
- [[CulinaryDB]]: "DACH Countries" 487 recipes shared with [[Austria]] and [[Switzerland]] (ingredients, partial amounts). [[IndicRecipeNutri]]: 63 "German" recipes.
- Dish inventories: [[WorldCuisines]] 77 dishes (29 Germany-only; Lebkuchen, Aachener Printen, Bethmännchen); [[FmLAMA]] 70 dishes (909 rows; Baumkuchen, Black Forest cake, Bauernfrühstück); [[World Wide Dishes]] 4 (1 Germany-only; e.g. Käsespätzle, Toast Hawaii). Ingredient lists only.

## Ingredients & food composition
- No national food composition table for this country is in the vault or `Backlog/`; use [[USDA FoodData Central]] (and [[FooDB]] / [[Phenol-Explorer]] for compounds) by name matching. The German Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel (BLS) is not a candidate.
- Plants: [[CMAUP]] 217 plants on the map; [[NPASS]] 49 species–compound pairs collected in Germany.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- **Human evidence:** [[Exposome-Explorer]] 1,644 rows (640 concentrations, 87 correlations, 907 reproducibility, 10 cancer associations), from GerES, Sentinel Public Health Offices, the Duisburg Birth Cohort, DONALD, ESTHER and EPIC-Germany. Useful for normal biomarker ranges; few diet → cancer rows.
- Traditional use: [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] ETHNOBOT 106 records (95 taxa); [[KNApSAcK Family]] World lists the country (edible/medicinal species records; counts for European countries were not scraped). German herbal medicine (Commission E monographs) is not in the vault.
- Global backbone via [[Global]]: [[FooDB]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[CTD]], [[SpiceRx]], [[DDID]] for food–drug interactions.

## Gaps
- No German FCT (BLS), no everyday-knowledge QA (no BLEnD file) and no German-language recipe corpus.
- German phytotherapy (Commission E) has no structured dataset in the vault.
