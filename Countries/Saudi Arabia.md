# Saudi Arabia

> [!summary] Data available
> The best-covered GCC country: the [[Saudi Food Composition Tables]] give 130 lab-analysed traditional dishes from all 13 regions with gram-level recipes, steps and 49 nutrients, the strongest Q1/Q2 source in the region. Everyday food knowledge comes from [[BLEnD]] (105 food questions) and [[ArabCulture]] (53 food items); compounds and traditional medicine rely on plant-occurrence resources ([[CMAUP]], [[KNApSAcK Family]]) and the [[Global]] backbones.

## Foods & dishes
- [[Saudi Food Composition Tables]] (SFDA): **130 dishes, all Saudi**, each with ingredients in g/ml (1,154 ingredient lines), numbered preparation steps and **49 analytes** per 100 g and per full recipe (lab-analysed, 3 preparations per dish).
  - By region: Makkah 22 · Al-Madinah 13 · Al-Baha 13 · Al-Jawf 12 · Tabuk 11 · Riyadh 10 · Najran 9 · Eastern 8 · Jazan 8 · Al-Qassim 7 · Aseer 7 · Hail 5 · Northern Borders 5. Dishes such as Qursan, Al-Jareesh, Kabsa, Saleeg, Mutabbaq, Hassawi rice, Kleja, Haneeth, Marsah.
  - Regional ingredients appear in the lines: ghee (70), whole/cracked wheat (56), lamb (37), cardamom (25), dates (16), dried black lime (12), camel meat (4), samh seeds (4), desert truffle (2).
  - Caveat: parsed from PDF; 24 dishes have fewer than 5 parsed ingredients (check `recipe_page`). The searchable SFDA web tool is geo-blocked.
- [[BLEnD]] (`Arabic_SaudiArabia`, SemEval-2026 file): 105 food questions, 420 food answer clusters. Everyday knowledge, no ingredients.
- [[ArabCulture]]: 261 items, 53 in the Food topic (21 Saudi-specific) + 8 food-adjacent. MCQs, no ingredients.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 24 dishes, but only 5 Saudi-specific; the rest are shared Middle-East dishes.
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: 102 user-tagged recipes (ingredients without amounts, steps, nutrition; often Americanised).
- [[RecipeDB2]]: ≈100 recipes labelled Saudi (amounts, steps, nutrition), but we hold only sample recipes.
- [[FmLAMA]]: 4 dishes (12 rows), e.g. `kleicha`, `Qassami Tawa`, with ingredient lists but no amounts.
- [[World Wide Dishes]]: 2 dishes, none Saudi-only (e.g. shawarma, `roz belkhalta` shared with Egypt).
- [[ArSyra Food and Culture]]: Saudi Arabia is claimed for the commercial full product; counts unknown.

## Ingredients & food composition
- **Own FCT (dishes):** the [[Saudi Food Composition Tables]] are dish-level: composition is measured for the cooked dish, not for raw ingredients.
- **Raw ingredients** must be borrowed: [[USDA FoodData Central]] or [[FooDB]] by name matching. Regional staples (camel meat/milk, samh, desert truffle, dried black lime) are unlikely to match well.
- Unprocessed candidates:
  - [[Arabic myfood24 FCDB]]: candidate, commercial; Arabic-named foods with nutrients for Saudi Arabia and Kuwait.
  - [[Saudi Branded Food Database]]: candidate, unavailable (SFDA branded foods).
  - [[EMFID (Eastern Mediterranean Food Information Data Bank)|EMFID]]: candidate, priority 1, open-web multi-country Arabic FCDB.
  - [[SaudiCulture]]: candidate, contact-authors; Saudi regional food QA (5 regions), no public data link.

## Compounds & traditional medicine
- No Saudi traditional-medicine database (Arabic / Prophetic medicine) is in the vault. The closest structured source for the shared Greco-Arabic humoral tradition is [[UNaProd]] (Persian; e.g. its black seed monograph).
- Plant occurrence: [[CMAUP]] 62 plants on the map (59 scraped); [[KNApSAcK Family]] 71 records (53 edible / 18 medicinal); [[NPASS]] 4 species–compound pairs with a Saudi collection site; [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] only 1 ethnobotany record.
- For compound → effect chains use the [[Global]] backbones ([[FooDB]], [[CTD]], [[Phenol-Explorer]], [[SpiceRx]], [[DDID]] for food–drug interactions); [[FooDB]] has **no nigella/black seed, sumac, camel milk, za'atar, ghee or labneh**, which are core Saudi ingredients.

## Gaps
- No raw-ingredient Saudi FCT and no Saudi recipe corpus with amounts beyond the 130 SFCT dishes.
- No Saudi or Arabic traditional-medicine / herbal-use dataset; Gulf ethnobotany is nearly absent from Duke.
- Compound data for camel milk, dates (by variety), black lime, samh and nigella must come from literature.
- The SFDA web database (possibly more foods than the book) is not reachable from our location.
