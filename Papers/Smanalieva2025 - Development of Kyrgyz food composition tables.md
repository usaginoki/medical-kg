# Development of Kyrgyz food composition tables: Limitations and challenges

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> This paper reports how Kyrgyzstan's first national food composition table was built, with support from the Slovak Republic's Official Development Assistance FCDB team. It covers **41 raw foods and products and 11 cooked Kyrgyz national dishes**, with complete amino-acid profiles for 3 foods and fatty-acid profiles for 4. The paper is closed access, so this note is based on the abstract (ScienceDirect) plus the FCT book itself (figshare, 2022), which the paper describes.

## What was built
- **Dataset(s):** [[Kyrgyzstan Food Composition Table]]
- **Sources & construction:**
  - Most macronutrient values are original laboratory analyses from Kyrgyz institutions, compiled with EuroFIR standards and documented in Daris 1.1.8 software.
  - Micronutrients are included where data were available.
  - Missing protein, fat and ash values were filled from the Russian food database and USDA (per the book).
- **Size & coverage:**
  - 41 raw foods: 2 cereals, 2 vegetables, 21 fruits, 2 nuts, 2 meats, 6 dairy, 3 fats, 2 beverages, 1 other.
  - 11 cooked national dishes, calculated from recipes with retention and yield factors.
  - Emphasis on ethnic foods (koumiss, bozo, chuchuk), domestic plant varieties and wild fruits.
- **Evaluation / applications:** the paper discusses limitations and challenges (full text not read).

## Key findings
1. Before this project there were **no food composition data reflecting Kyrgyz food products** (Highlights).
2. The first edition covers **41 raw foods + 11 cooked national dishes**. Components: protein, available and by-difference carbohydrate, fat and ash per 100 g edible portion, plus amino acids for 3 foods and fatty acids for 4.
3. Most macronutrient values are **original chemical analyses**. Vitamins and minerals are included depending on data availability.
4. The authors call for continued development, regular updates and national/international data exchange.

## Relevance to research questions
### Q1: Cultural food datasets
The 11 dish recipes (beshbarmak, plov with barberry, manty, lagman, oromo, shorpo, mastava…) give ingredient grams and calculated nutrients for Kyrgyz national dishes. This is the only such dish-level data for Central Asia in the vault.

See [[Q1 Cultural food datasets]]

### Q2: Cultural ingredient datasets
The raw-food table covers local ingredients rarely found in global tables: koumiss, mare/yak/hainak milk, yak and horse fat, Ozgon rice, and wild *Malus sieversii*, sea buckthorn, barberry and cherry plum. Scientific names allow linking to compound databases.

See [[Q2 Cultural ingredient datasets]]

## Limitations / caveats
- The full text was not accessible (Elsevier, closed access). Findings are limited to the abstract and highlights; the book supplied the detailed numbers.
- The table is small, with sparse micronutrients, and the dish values are calculated rather than analysed.
