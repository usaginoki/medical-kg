4.68 million food–health claims, but only 1 in 20 is characteristic

The graph goes from a dish to its ingredients, to their compounds, to conditions and drugs. A claim is characteristic when the food really carries it: every direct claim counts, but a compound path counts only if the food has at least 1 mg/100 g of a rare compound or is a major source of it.

Cues are keyword rules, so some are false hits: “denied recent travel” in a MedCaseReasoning case still counts as a place cue. Since 15 Oct, alcohol and food-triggered symptoms count as diet, and parental consanguinity is no longer a cue.

LLM check: does culture change the answer?

Gemini Flash-Lite read the 21,636 cases the keyword screen handed on (MedArabiQ excluded) and tagged each cue as decisive, supportive, incidental or none, with a one-sentence reason. Partner’s full run of 15 Oct 2026; numbers from LLM tagging experiments. The result files are not on this machine.

The note flags the FAM-Bench and NGQA “decisive” tags as unreliable: the model often confuses “diet matters” with “culture matters”.
