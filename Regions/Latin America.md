# Latin America

> [!summary] Data available
> Latin America and the Caribbean are covered unevenly: [[Mexico]] is well covered (13 datasets, large recipe sets, a BLEnD culture, 5,384 Duke records), a handful of South American and Caribbean countries have dish-level coverage, and most small islands have only a few shared WorldCuisines dishes or a KNApSAcK map page. No national FCT for any Latin American country is in the vault or the Backlog, so composition falls back to [[USDA FoodData Central]].

## Region-wide datasets
- Region labels without a country split: [[CulinaryDB]] `Caribbean` 1,103, `South America` 310, `Central America` 14; [[RecipeDB2]] v1 `South American` 7,176, `Caribbean` 3,026 (of which "Rest Caribbean" ≈2,200), `Central American` 460; [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] `central-american` 1,818, `caribbean` 1,709, `south-american` 1,255.
- [[WorldCuisines]] cuisine labels: `Latin American` 5, `Caribbean` 8, `South American` 3, `Central American` 3.
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] has 526 region-only Latin America records (South/Central America, West Indies, Guiana) on top of the per-country ones.
- Backlog: [[RecetasDeLaAbuela]] (priority 3, open-download), Spanish-language recipes labelled by Spanish-speaking country; the only candidate aimed at the region.

## Gaps
- No national FCT for any country (e.g. Brazil's TACO, Mexico's tables, LATINFOODS) is in the vault or the Backlog; USDA is a poor proxy for Andean, Amazonian and Caribbean staples.
- Apart from Mexico, recipe data are small, often diaspora-biased (US uploaders) and not released with amounts ([[RecipeDB2]]); there is no Portuguese-language corpus.
- Traditional medicine rests on Duke ethnobotany records; there is no curated Latin American herbal database (plant → indication → dose). The French, Dutch and British Caribbean territories have no data at all.
