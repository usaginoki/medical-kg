# Oceania

> [!summary] Data available
> Weak outside Australia and New Zealand. Australia (recipes, a [[BLEnD]] file, [[Exposome-Explorer]] cohorts) and New Zealand (recipes, dish lists) are covered for mainstream Anglo food; the Pacific Islands have almost no food data: Fiji and Palau get a few dishes, and Samoa, Tonga, the Solomon Islands and Fiji are visible only through [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] ethnobotany. Fourteen territories have only a [[KNApSAcK Family]] flag page. No Oceanian food composition table is in the vault.

## Region-wide datasets
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: `south-west-pacific` 3,934 recipes (mostly Australian/NZ), `polynesian` 203, `micro-melanesia` 49. The last two are the only recipe signal for the Pacific Islands (user tags on an American site).
- [[RecipeDB2]]: v1 region "Australian" 5,823 recipes (Australia ≈4,680, New Zealand ≈1,120); samples only held.
- [[CulinaryDB]]: "Australia & NZ" 494 recipes, not split.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: region labels "Australia and New Zealand" 33 dishes and "Melanesia" 4; no Polynesia or Micronesia label.
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: Pacific ethnobotany (Samoa, Tonga, Solomon Islands, Fiji, plus raw rows for Guam, Tahiti, Niue, New Caledonia, Caroline Islands); 341 Hawaii rows are counted under the United States.
- [[Exposome-Explorer]] and [[BLEnD]] reach only Australia (and NZ for Exposome-Explorer).

## Gaps
- No food composition table: neither the Australian AFCD/AUSNUT, the NZ Food Composition Database nor the Pacific Islands Food Composition Tables are in the vault or `Backlog/`.
- Pacific Island food cultures (iTaukei, Samoan, Tongan, Melanesian, Micronesian) have no dish, recipe or QA dataset; Indigenous Australian and Māori food and medicine are barely present.
- Key Pacific foods and remedies (kava, noni, pandanus) have no [[FooDB]] entry; taro, breadfruit, cassava, coconut and sago do.
