# South Asia

> [!summary] Data available
> South Asia is dominated by India, which has a state-labelled recipe corpus with amounts and nutrition and three Ayurveda/Siddha/Unani knowledge bases; every other country has only dish names, a few hundred recipes at most, and plant lists. No national FCT in the region is processed (IFCT 2017, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal and Sri Lanka tables are all in the Backlog), and Bhutan and the Maldives are nearly empty.

## Region-wide datasets
- Region labels without a country split: [[CulinaryDB]] "Indian Subcontinent" 4,058 recipes (mostly TarlaDalal, so largely Indian); [[RecipeDB2]] "Indian Subcontinent" 6,464 (v1); [[WorldCuisines]] "Indian Subcontinent" 15 and "South Asian" 5 dishes (Region1 Southern Asia 200).
- Traditional-medicine system labels that cross borders: [[IMPPAT]] and [[CMAUP]] (Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, Sowa Rigpa); [[UNaProd]] (Persian medicine, labelled South Asia for Unani).
- Backlog: [[South Asian Recipes with Nutrition]] (Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Afghanistan; possibly synthetic; priority 3).

## Gaps
- No processed FCT anywhere in the region: [[Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017)]] (priority 1, site down), [[Pakistan Food Composition Table]], [[Bangladesh Food Composition Table]], [[Nepal Food Composition Table]], [[Sri Lanka Food Composition Database]] are all candidates.
- Outside India there is no recipe corpus with amounts; Pakistani and Bangladeshi dishes are folded into "Indian Subcontinent" labels.
- [[BLEnD]] covers only Assam and Tamil Sri Lanka; no Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal culture.
- Unani and Sowa Rigpa are covered only via India-based plant databases; no Pakistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan or Sri Lanka traditional-medicine source.
