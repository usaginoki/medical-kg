# Sub-Saharan Africa

> [!summary] Data available
> Sub-Saharan Africa is covered almost only at dish level. [[World Wide Dishes]] (501 of its 765 dishes tagged Africa, community-written with local-language names and eating context) makes [[Nigeria]] (88), [[Kenya]] (85), [[South Africa]] (81), [[Cameroon]] (47) and [[Uganda]] (40) the best-covered countries; [[Ethiopia]] and Northern Nigeria have [[BLEnD]] files. There is no FCT, no recipe corpus with usable amounts and no African traditional-medicine database in the vault; the West African FCT (WAFCT) sits unprocessed inside the [[FAO INFOODS global databases]] candidate. Seven countries have only 1–3 datasets (Réunion and South Sudan just one).

## Region-wide datasets
- [[World Wide Dishes]]: 501 dishes tagged Africa (incl. North Africa). Six countries have 30-dish image test sets, four of them Sub-Saharan (Nigeria, Cameroon, South Africa, Kenya; plus Algeria and the US), with community image-review rows (South Africa 1,495, Cameroon 1,380, Nigeria 1,212, Kenya 450).
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: `african` tag 2,851 recipes (ingredients without amounts, steps, nutrition); `congolese` 11 (which Congo is unclear).
- [[RecipeDB2]]: "Rest Africa" 2,740 recipes in v1, most labelled Nigerian (≈2,520), so country labels are unreliable; not released.
- [[CulinaryDB]]: "Africa" 651 recipes, not split into North and Sub-Saharan Africa.
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 1,135 ethnobotany rows labelled only "Africa"; country rows are mostly from Ayensu (West Africa) and Guillarmod (Lesotho).
- [[KNApSAcK Family]]: all African countries are on its World map, but African flag pages were not scraped.
- [[Exposome-Explorer]]: only [[Benin]] (18 values) and [[Gambia]] (1).
- **Unprocessed candidate:** [[FAO INFOODS global databases]] (priority 2), whose directory includes the West African Food Composition Table (WAFCT) for the ECOWAS countries: the single most useful Q2 addition for the region.

## Gaps
- No food composition table for any Sub-Saharan country is processed; WAFCT is the obvious first step, and East, Central and Southern Africa have no candidate at all.
- No recipe corpus with amounts: RecipeDB2's African labels are unreleased and unreliable, Food.com's are few and Americanised.
- No African traditional-medicine database (e.g. West African or Southern African herbal medicine); Duke is patchy (Lesotho 1,051 vs Kenya 20) and KNApSAcK Africa was not scraped.
- Central Africa beyond Cameroon and the DRC, the Sahel, the Horn outside Ethiopia and the Indian Ocean islands have almost no food data.
