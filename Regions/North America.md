# North America

> [!summary] Data available
> North America is the most heavily covered region in the vault, almost entirely through the [[United States]], which is the home culture of [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]], [[CulinaryDB]] and [[USDA FoodData Central]] and leads [[WorldCuisines]], [[FmLAMA]] and [[Exposome-Explorer]]. [[Canada]] is well covered for recipes but has no FCT of its own; [[Greenland]] has essentially nothing.

## Countries
Ranked by coverage:
1. [[United States]]: very strong. Food.com 31,596 recipes, CulinaryDB 16,118, IndicRecipeNutri `American` 12,718, FmLAMA 285 and WorldCuisines 216 dishes, BLEnD `US`; national FCT [[USDA FoodData Central]]; Exposome-Explorer 7,553 rows; Duke 4,964 records. Beware the US-default bias of these sources.
2. [[Canada]]: good for recipes, weak otherwise. Food.com 4,572, RecipeDB2 `Canadian` 6,700 (not released), CulinaryDB 1,112, WorldCuisines 43; Exposome-Explorer 740 rows; no FCT (USDA as proxy).
3. [[Greenland]]: minimal. KNApSAcK map page only; CMAUP 13 plants listed under Denmark.

## Region-wide datasets
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] region tag `north-american` 48,479 recipes (US, Canada and Mexico mixed).
- [[CulinaryDB]] labels `USA` (16,118) and `Canada` (1,112) separately; [[RecipeDB2]] has `US` (5,031) and `Canadian` (6,700) regions.
- [[USDA FoodData Central]] is US data but is the de facto composition fallback for the whole vault (and for Latin America); [[RecipeDB2]] ingredients are keyed to USDA SR Legacy ids.
- Backlog candidates for the US: [[USDA Flavonoid, Isoflavone and Proanthocyanidin Databases]], [[NIH DSLD]], [[NaPDI Center]], [[iDISK]].

## Gaps
- **US-default bias:** the largest "global" recipe and composition sources are American, so statistics drawn from them over-represent US ingredients and portions; other cultures need re-weighting or their own sources.
- No Canadian Nutrient File and no Indigenous (First Nations, Inuit, Native American) food dataset beyond 164 Food.com recipes and 165 USDA Alaska Native foods.
- Regional US cuisines (Southern, Cajun, Tex-Mex, Hawaiian) exist only as Food.com user tags without amounts.
