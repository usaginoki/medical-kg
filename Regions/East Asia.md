# East Asia

> [!summary] Data available
> East Asia is the best-covered region in the vault. China and Japan both have large native-language recipe corpora, rich dish inventories and strong compound data; Japan and South Korea have processed national FCTs; China has four processed TCM databases and TM-MC covers Korean and Kampo materials. Taiwan, North Korea, Hong Kong and Macau have little beyond BLEnD cultures and dish names.

## Region-wide datasets
- [[TM-MC]] spans Korea (649 materials), China (572) and Japan (195) in one material → compound → protein → disease graph.
- [[RecipeDB2]] "Chinese and Mongolian" 5,896 (v1) mixes China and Mongolia; [[WorldCuisines]] Region1 Eastern Asia 420 dishes; [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] tag `asian` 13,485 (whole of Asia).
- [[Exposome-Explorer]]: East Asian cohorts (JPHC, Shanghai cohorts, Korea, Taiwan) give food → biomarker → cancer evidence.

## Gaps
- China has no processed FCT or intake survey ([[China Food Composition Tables]], [[China Health and Nutrition Survey (CHNS)]] priority 1); Korea lacks dish-level composition and intake data ([[MFDS Food Nutrient DB (Korea)]], [[KNHANES]] priority 1).
- No TCM database flags food herbs: [[China Medicine-Food Homology List]] (priority 1) is needed to join TCM to recipes.
- Sub-national Chinese cuisines are nearly unlabelled ([[FoodieQA]] with 14 regional cuisines is a candidate).
- Kampo is thin ([[KampoDB]] candidate) and there is no Korean-language recipe corpus.
- Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau have no FCT, processed or in the Backlog.
