# Central Asia

> [!summary] Data available
> Thin but improving, thanks to ISSAI (Astana): the [[Central Asian Food Dataset]] (dish images) and the [[Central Asian Digital Visual Food Atlas]] (portion weights, names in 5 languages) cover the five former-Soviet republics at regional level, and the [[Kyrgyzstan Food Composition Table]] is the only national FCT. No country has a recipe corpus with a country label; the Caucasus (Azerbaijan, Armenia, Georgia), Afghanistan and Mongolia have only knowledge-base dishes and plant counts, and Azerbaijan alone has a [[BLEnD]] file.

## Region-wide datasets
- [[Central Asian Food Dataset]]: CAFD 42 national dish classes (16,402 images on HF), CAFSD 21,306 meal-scene photos / 239 classes / 69,865 boxes; no per-image country, no recipes or nutrients.
- [[Central Asian Digital Visual Food Atlas]]: 115 items (95 foods × 3 portion weights, 20 beverages), local names in Kazakh, Uzbek, Kyrgyz, Tajik, Turkmen.
- [[Kyrgyzstan Food Composition Table]]: its 11 dishes (beshbarmak, plov, manty, lagman, shorpo, mastava) are shared across the region; combined with atlas weights they give nutrients per plate.
- [[WorldCuisines]]: 9 dishes with the cuisine label "Central Asian" (e.g. plov → Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Kazakhstan).
- Recipe corpora ([[RecipeDB2]], [[CulinaryDB]], [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]) have **no Central Asia label**; Duke has 1 "Turkistan" record.
- **Unprocessed candidates:** [[ISSAI LLM for Dietary Recommendation System]], [[ISSAI Food Portion Benchmark]], [[ISSAI Global Gastronomic Culinary Dataset]] (all candidates, open-download, from ISSAI); [[FooDI-ML]] (candidate, menu items incl. Kazakhstan).

## Gaps
- **Composition:** only Kyrgyzstan (41 raw foods, 11 dishes); nothing for Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, the Caucasus, Afghanistan or Mongolia.
- **Recipes with amounts:** only the 11 Kyrgyz dishes.
- **Everyday knowledge:** no BLEnD or similar QA for the five republics (Azerbaijan only).
- **Traditional medicine:** no Central Asian, Caucasian or Mongolian traditional-medicine dataset; plant counts only ([[CMAUP]], [[KNApSAcK Family]]); CMAUP has no entry for Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan or Armenia.
- **Compounds of pastoral foods:** [[FooDB]] has no camel milk (shubat); koumiss, horse meat and kurt need checking.
