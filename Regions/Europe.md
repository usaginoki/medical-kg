# Europe

> [!summary] Data available
> Europe is the best-covered non-priority region for dishes and recipes: France, Italy, Spain, the United Kingdom, Germany and Greece each have hundreds to thousands of labelled recipes ([[RecipeDB2]], [[CulinaryDB]], [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]) and large dish inventories ([[FmLAMA]], [[WorldCuisines]]), and seven countries have a [[BLEnD]] file. It is also the core of the human-evidence layer: [[Exposome-Explorer]] (IARC) draws heavily on European cohorts (EPIC, SU.VI.MAX, E3N, ATBC, MoBa, NSHDC, PREDIMED, TwinsUK), and [[Phenol-Explorer]] was built by INRA with IARC. The big hole is composition: **no European national food composition table** (CIQUAL, BLS, CoFID, BEDCA, NEVO, Fineli, Frida) is in the vault or `Backlog/`, so nutrients fall back to [[USDA FoodData Central]]; the Balkans, Baltics, Belarus, Moldova, Ukraine and the micro-states have only dish names.

## Region-wide datasets
- [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]]: region tag `european` 24,912 recipes and `scandinavian` 1,294 (country tags also keep Scottish 715 and Welsh 203 apart from English 1,744).
- [[RecipeDB2]] (v1 regions): Italian 16,582 · French 6,381 · UK 4,401 · Deutschland 4,323 (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) · Greek 4,185 · Spanish and Portuguese 2,844 · Scandinavian 2,811 · Irish 2,532 · Eastern European 2,503 (Russia, Poland, Hungary, Czech Republic + 100 "Rest Eastern European") · Belgian 1,060 (incl. Dutch). Not released; we hold samples.
- [[CulinaryDB]]: British Isles 1,075, Eastern Europe 565, DACH 487, Scandinavia 404 (no country split), besides country labels for Italy, France, Greece, Spain, Portugal, Netherlands and Belgium.
- [[IndicRecipeNutri]]: `European` 303 and `Mediterranean` 169 recipes (Indian sites), besides Italian 978, British 950, French 650, Spanish 115, Greek 107, German 63.
- [[BLEnD]]: 7 European countries with 105 food questions each: [[Spain]] (2 files), [[United Kingdom]], [[Greece]], [[France]], [[Ireland]], [[Sweden]], [[Bulgaria]].
- [[Exposome-Explorer]]: European cohorts dominate the human-evidence layer. EPIC (European Prospective Investigation into Cancer and Nutrition) appears for France, Spain, the UK, the Netherlands, Italy, Germany, Greece, Norway and Sweden; national cohorts add SU.VI.MAX/E3N/NutriNet-Santé (France), ATBC/KIHD (Finland), MoBa (Norway), NSHDC/Malmö (Sweden), DCH/Inter99/OPUS (Denmark), PREDIMED (Spain), TwinsUK/Whitehall (UK), InCHIANTI/ORDET (Italy).
- [[Phenol-Explorer]]: INRA/IARC polyphenol composition of 458 generic foods; no country labels.
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]]: 998 ETHNOBOT rows labelled only "Europe", 67 "Mediterranean", 95 "USSR", 171 "Eurasia"; plus language-word rows (German 235, English 233, French 233, Dutch 168, Spanish 136, Italian 129, Portuguese 42, Danish 20) that are common names, not places.
- [[CMAUP]]: plant counts for most Western/Southern European countries (Italy 378, Russia 360, Spain 281, France 258 …) but only 2–3 plants for Croatia, Estonia and the Czech Republic, and no entry for Latvia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Moldova or the ex-Yugoslav states other than Croatia.
- **Candidates:** [[eBASIS]] (EU bioactive-compound database with human-trial effects; unavailable), [[RecetasDeLaAbuela]] (Spanish-speaking recipes), [[FAO INFOODS global databases]] (directory of national tables, the route to European FCTs).

## Gaps
- **Composition:** no European national FCT (CIQUAL, BLS, CoFID, BEDCA, NEVO, Fineli, Frida, CREA) in the vault or `Backlog/`; add candidates via [[FAO INFOODS global databases]] or EuroFIR.
- **Recipes with amounts:** RecipeDB2 has them but is unreleased; downloadable European recipes (CulinaryDB, Food.com) lack full amounts or are Americanised; no native-language European recipe corpus.
- **Eastern Europe and the Balkans:** Ukraine, Belarus, Moldova, the ex-Yugoslav states and Albania have only knowledge-base dish names; no QA, no recipes, no cohort data.
- **Traditional / herbal medicine:** no European phytotherapy dataset (Commission E, EMA herbal monographs); Duke has few records per country outside Spain, the UK, France and the Netherlands; [[eBASIS]] is offline.
