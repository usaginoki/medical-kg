# Middle East

> [!summary] Data available
> Uneven. Saudi Arabia is the only country with a full dish-level source (130 lab-analysed dishes with recipes, steps and 49 nutrients); Iran, Turkey and Lebanon have many dishes and recipes but no FCT in the vault; Iran alone has a traditional-medicine database ([[UNaProd]]). The other GCC states (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman) are nearly empty beyond shared knowledge-base dishes, and Bahrain has a mostly borrowed FCT. Everyday food knowledge is good for Arab countries through [[ArabCulture]] (not Iraq or the Gulf beyond Saudi/UAE/Yemen).

## Region-wide datasets
- **Recipes with a region label only:** [[RecipeDB2]] "Middle Eastern" 3,905 recipes (v1), of which ≈2,300 are "Rest Middle Eastern" with no country (incl. Iranian/Persian); [[CulinaryDB]] Middle East 993 recipes (no country split); [[Food.com Recipes and Interactions]] `middle-eastern` 2,067 + `ramadan` 279; [[IndicRecipeNutri]] Middle Eastern 388 (+ Mediterranean 169).
- **Region-level dish labels:** [[WorldCuisines]] cuisines Middle Eastern 14, Levantine 14, Arab/Arabic 7, Persian 2, Arabian Peninsula 1; Gulf dishes are mostly shared across all GCC states.
- **Pan-Arab QA:** [[ArabCulture]] (Gulf / Levant / Nile Valley / North Africa, 724 food items over 13 countries); [[ArSyra Food and Culture]] (commercial; 50 Syrian preview records).
- **Plants:** [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] has 201 records with Middle-East region labels (Kurdistan 134, Arabic 24, Arab 23, Arabia 16, Jerusalem 4).
- **Unprocessed candidates:** [[EMFID (Eastern Mediterranean Food Information Data Bank)|EMFID]] (candidate, priority 1, multi-country Arabic FCDB); [[MENA-150]] (candidate, 150 MENA dish classes, 53k images); [[Arabic myfood24 FCDB]] (candidate, commercial); [[Palm (Arabic cultural instructions)]] and [[ArabCulture-Dialogue]] (candidates, dialectal Arabic incl. dish names); [[FooDI-ML]] (candidate, menu items).

## Gaps
- **GCC beyond Saudi Arabia:** no recipes and no own composition for UAE, Qatar, Kuwait or Oman; Bahrain's FCT is mostly borrowed.
- **No FCT processed for Iran, Turkey or the Levant**; candidates exist ([[Iranian Food Composition Table]], [[TürKomp]], [[Lebanon traditional dishes composition]], EMFID).
- **Traditional medicine:** only Persian ([[UNaProd]]); no Arabic / Prophetic / Gulf herbal-medicine dataset; Duke's Gulf coverage is near zero and its Turkish records are probably language labels.
- **Compounds of regional staples:** [[FooDB]] lacks nigella/black seed, sumac, camel milk, za'atar, ghee and labneh; dates by variety, dried black lime and desert truffle are not covered by any compound source here.
- **Everyday knowledge:** [[BLEnD]] has only Saudi Arabia and Iran (plus Egypt); ArabCulture misses Iraq, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman.
