# Southeast Asia

> [!summary] Data available
> Southeast Asia is covered mainly by multi-country dish inventories, a few thousand English-language recipes and plant knowledge bases; no country has a processed FCT or a native-language recipe corpus. Indonesia, Thailand and the Philippines are the best covered; Brunei and Timor-Leste have only dish names.

## Region-wide datasets
- [[CulinaryDB]] "South East Asia" 611 recipes (no country split); [[RecipeDB2]] region "Southeast Asian" 1,940 (v1; countries partly split); [[WorldCuisines]] Region1 South-Eastern Asia 362 dishes, many shared across Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia and Brunei.
- [[Dr. Duke's Phytochemical and Ethnobotanical Databases|Duke]] region-only labels for Southeast Asia (Indochina, Borneo, East Indies) 316 records.
- Backlog: [[ASEAN Food Composition Database]] (regional 2014; priority 2), the fallback for countries without their own table.

## Gaps
- No processed FCT anywhere: [[Indonesian Food Composition Data (TKPI)]], [[Thai Food Composition Database]], [[Malaysian Food Composition Database]], [[Philippine Food Composition Tables]], [[Vietnamese Food Composition Table]] are candidates; Singapore, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Brunei, Timor-Leste have none even in the Backlog.
- No native-language recipe corpus processed ([[Indonesian Food Recipes]] is a candidate); all recipes are English, mostly Western adaptations.
- Traditional medicine is covered only for Jamu (samples) and via plant lists; [[IJAH Analytics]] and [[VIETHERB]] are candidates, and there is nothing for Thai, Filipino, Malay or Burmese traditional medicine.
- BLEnD covers only Indonesia, Singapore and the Philippines.
