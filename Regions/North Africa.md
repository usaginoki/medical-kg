# North Africa

> [!summary] Data available
> Moderate, mostly QA and dish-list level. Egypt, Morocco and Algeria are best covered ([[ArabCulture]] for all Arab states, [[BLEnD]] for Egypt, Morocco and Algeria, [[World Wide Dishes]] strong for Algeria and Egypt), and Morocco has a large recipe set. No North African FCT is in the vault, and Mauritania and Western Sahara are nearly empty.

## Countries

2. [[Morocco]]: **good recipes**. RecipeDB2 1,560, Food.com 897; BLEnD file (199 food answer clusters), ArabCulture 33 specific food items; KNApSAcK 263, NPASS 248, CMAUP 184.

## Region-wide datasets
- [[RecipeDB2]]: "Northern Africa" 1,611 recipes (v1), mostly Moroccan.
- [[CulinaryDB]]: "Africa" 651 recipes, not split into North and Sub-Saharan Africa.
- [[ArabCulture]]: Nile Valley (Egypt, Sudan) and North Africa (Algeria, Libya, Morocco, Tunisia) groups.
- **Unprocessed candidates:** [[MENA-150]] (candidate, dish images incl. North Africa), [[EMFID (Eastern Mediterranean Food Information Data Bank)|EMFID]] (candidate, Eastern Mediterranean Arab FCDB), [[Palm (Arabic cultural instructions)]] (candidate, 22 Arab countries).

## Gaps
- No North African FCT processed; Egypt's is print only.
- No traditional-medicine dataset (e.g. Moroccan or Egyptian herbal medicine) beyond plant-occurrence and Duke records.
- Recipes with amounts exist only as RecipeDB2 samples; Mauritania and Western Sahara have almost nothing.

*Maghreb countries:* Morocco is the only one with sizeable recipe sets (RecipeDB2 ≈1,560, Food.com 897; Libya has just 40 and ≈20); Algeria, Tunisia and Libya rely on dish lists and [[ArabCulture]] MCQs, Sudan has the strongest ethnobotany (Duke 351 records) but almost no recipes, and Mauritania and Western Sahara are not in ArabCulture at all. None has an FCT, and Maghreb herbal medicine is visible only through [[KNApSAcK Family]]'s 109 medicinal Moroccan records and a handful of Duke rows.
